Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Postador ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you authorize it to access your Instagram account via the Meta Platform.

1. What the App Does

Postador is a personal automation tool that generates short video content and publishes it to an Instagram account using the Instagram Graph API. The App operates on behalf of a single authorized account owner and is not a public consumer service.

2. Data We Access

To publish content, the App requests the following permissions from Meta:

  • instagram_basic — read basic account information (username, account ID).
  • instagram_content_publish — upload photos and videos to your Instagram account.

No other permissions are requested or used.

3. Data We Collect and Store

The App does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data with third parties. The only information retained locally is:

  • Access tokens issued by Meta, stored on the local machine of the authorized user only.
  • Generated media files (video/images) stored locally before upload, deleted after posting.

No analytics, tracking, or user databases are maintained.

4. How We Use Your Data

Data accessed through Meta APIs is used solely to:

  • Authenticate the App with your Instagram account.
  • Publish content (video + caption + hashtags) to your Instagram feed on your behalf.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share any data with any third party. The App communicates only with:

  • Meta's Graph API endpoints (graph.facebook.com), to publish content.
  • A local Ollama instance or the Anthropic API, to generate text — no Instagram data is sent to these services.

6. Data Retention

Access tokens are stored locally and can be deleted at any time by revoking the App's permissions in your Instagram / Facebook account settings or by removing the token files from your machine. No server-side retention exists.

7. User Rights

As the sole authorized user, you can at any time:

  • Revoke the App's access via Facebook App Settings.
  • Delete all locally stored tokens and media files from your machine.
  • Request information about what data the App has accessed by contacting us (see below).

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
