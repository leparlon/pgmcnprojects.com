Overview

Human OmegaPoster ("we", "our", or "the platform") is a content publishing and automation tool designed to help creators manage and distribute their original content across multiple social media platforms.

Information We Collect

When you connect a social media account, we may store:

  • Account identifiers provided through OAuth authentication.
  • Access tokens and refresh tokens required to interact with supported platforms.
  • Basic profile information made available by the platform.
  • Content metadata related to uploaded videos and posts.
  • Performance metrics and analytics data provided by supported platforms.

Human OmegaPoster does not collect passwords for third-party platforms.

How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Authenticate connected accounts.
  • Upload and manage content on behalf of the user.
  • Retrieve analytics and performance information.
  • Improve the publishing workflow and user experience.

Content Ownership

All uploaded content remains the property of the creator. Human OmegaPoster does not claim ownership of any user-generated content.

Sharing of Information

Human OmegaPoster does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties, except when required to provide functionality through connected platform APIs or when required by law.

Data Storage

Authentication tokens and related metadata are stored securely and are used only to provide the requested publishing and analytics functionality.

Third-Party Services

Human OmegaPoster integrates with third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • TikTok
  • Instagram
  • YouTube

Use of these services is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

Data Deletion

Users may revoke access to their connected accounts at any time through the corresponding platform settings. Upon request, stored account information and tokens may be deleted.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information and prevent unauthorized access. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Human OmegaPoster is not intended for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
