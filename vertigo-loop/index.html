Vertigo Loop

A private content-automation tool operated by PGMCN Projects for its own YouTube channel.

Vertigo Loop uses the YouTube Data API v3 to upload and manage videos on the operator's Vertigo Loop YouTube channel. Every upload and schedule is configured and authorized by the operator, who retains final control over the videos, their metadata, visibility, and authorization at all times.

Vertigo Loop is not offered as a public SaaS product. It does not provide public user registration, and it is not intended for use by anyone other than its operator.
